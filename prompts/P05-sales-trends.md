# P05 — Sales Trend Identification

## Version 1.0 — Initial Prompt

Analyse the supplied retail sales data and business
rules to identify increasing and decreasing product
sales trends.

## Intended Workflow

Weekly product-level sales trend analysis
to support management decision-making.

## Problem Being Solved

Manual trend analysis may delay the identification
of changing product sales patterns.

## Automation Potential

High for repetitive sales comparisons and
trend classification, subject to verification.

## Risks and Limitations

AI may use incorrect comparison periods,
miscalculate growth rates or make unsupported
forecasts and causal assumptions.
