# P06 — Stockout Risk Detection

## Version 1.0 — Initial Prompt

Analyse the supplied retail sales data, inventory
data and business rules to identify products
at risk of running out of stock.

## Intended Workflow

Weekly inventory risk assessment following
sales performance and trend analysis.

## Problem Being Solved

Manual inventory monitoring may delay the
identification of potential stockouts,
resulting in lost sales and reduced product
availability.

## Automation Potential

High for repetitive inventory calculations
and risk classification, subject to accurate
inventory records and human verification.

## Risks and Limitations

AI may miscalculate stock requirements,
ignore supplier lead times, misinterpret
safety stock or incorrectly classify
stockout risks.
