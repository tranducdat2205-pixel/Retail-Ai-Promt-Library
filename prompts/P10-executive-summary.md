# P10 — Executive Summary Report

## Version 1.0 — Initial Prompt

Review the supplied management recommendations
report and prepare a professional executive
summary for senior management.

## Intended Workflow

Final stage of the weekly retail analytics
workflow following P09 management recommendations.

## Problem Being Solved

Lengthy analytical reports may increase
information overload and delay senior
management decisions.

## Automation Potential

High for repetitive report summarisation
and structured executive communication.

Human review remains necessary to verify
material findings and decision requirements.

## Risks and Limitations

AI may omit critical information, distort
business metrics, oversimplify policy conflicts
or misrepresent proposed actions as approved
management decisions.
