# P08 — Replenishment Recommendations

## Version 1.0 — Initial Prompt

Review the supplied inventory data, business rules,
stockout risk report and overstock assessment to
recommend replenishment quantities for each product.

## Intended Workflow

Weekly replenishment planning following P06
stockout assessment and P07 overstock identification.

## Problem Being Solved

Manual replenishment planning may result in
insufficient stock, unnecessary purchasing
and inconsistent inventory decisions.

## Automation Potential

High for repetitive order quantity calculations.

Medium for operational decision-making because
conflicting inventory policies, supplier constraints
and purchasing decisions require human judgement.

## Risks and Limitations

AI may miscalculate order quantities, overlook
conflicting business rules or recommend unnecessary
purchases without sufficient operational evidence.
