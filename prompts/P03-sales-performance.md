# P03 — Sales Performance Analysis

## Version 1.0 — Initial Prompt

Analyse the supplied retail sales data and identify
the best-performing and worst-performing products
and categories.

## Intended Workflow

Weekly sales performance analysis following
data validation and standardisation.

## Problem Being Solved

Manual sales analysis is time-consuming and may
delay the identification of underperforming products.

## Automation Potential

AI-assisted sales analysis and performance
summarisation, subject to numerical verification.

## Risks and Limitations

AI may miscalculate revenue, use inconsistent
performance metrics or generate unsupported insights.
