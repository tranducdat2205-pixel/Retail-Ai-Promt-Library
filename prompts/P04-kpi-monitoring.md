# P04 — KPI Performance Monitoring

## Version 1.0 — Initial Prompt

Review the supplied retail sales performance
report and business rules to evaluate whether
the business is meeting its performance targets.

## Intended Workflow

Weekly KPI monitoring following P03 sales
performance analysis.

## Problem Being Solved

Manual KPI monitoring delays the identification
of performance gaps and management responses.

## Automation Potential

High for repetitive KPI comparisons and
performance exception reporting.

## Risks and Limitations

AI may miscalculate KPI variances, invent
performance targets or misinterpret business rules.
