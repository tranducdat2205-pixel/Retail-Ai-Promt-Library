# P02 — Retail Data Standardisation

## Version 1.0 — Initial Prompt

Clean and standardise the supplied retail sales data
using the identified data quality issues.

## Intended Workflow

Data preparation following P01 validation.

## Problem Being Solved

Inconsistent and incomplete records reduce the
reliability of sales and inventory reporting.

## Automation Potential

AI-assisted data cleaning and standardisation,
subject to human approval.

## Risks and Limitations

AI may incorrectly modify valid records, estimate
missing values or remove legitimate transactions.
