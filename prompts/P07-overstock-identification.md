# P07 — Overstock Identification

## Version 1.0 — Initial Prompt

Analyse the supplied retail sales data, inventory
data and business rules to identify overstocked
products.

## Intended Workflow

Weekly inventory monitoring to identify
excess stock and potential inventory inefficiencies.

## Problem Being Solved

Excess inventory may increase storage costs,
tie up working capital and create additional
markdown risks.

## Automation Potential

High for repetitive stock coverage calculations
and rule-based overstock classification.

## Risks and Limitations

AI may use incorrect demand periods, include
inbound inventory incorrectly, misinterpret
overstock thresholds or recommend unsupported
inventory actions.
