# P01 — Data Quality Validation

## Version 1.0 — Initial Prompt

Review the provided retail sales data and identify
any errors or inconsistencies.

## Intended Workflow

Weekly sales data validation before business analysis.

## Problem Being Solved

Manual data checking is time-consuming and prone
to human error.

## Automation Potential

AI-assisted identification of data quality issues,
subject to human verification.

## Risks and Limitations

AI may overlook errors or misclassify valid records.
