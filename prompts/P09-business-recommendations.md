# P09 — Business Action Recommendations

## Version 1.0 — Initial Prompt

Review the supplied retail KPI, sales trend,
stockout, overstock and replenishment reports
to recommend business actions for management.

## Intended Workflow

Weekly management decision support following
sales performance and inventory analysis.

## Problem Being Solved

Fragmented analytical reports may delay
management decisions and make it difficult
to prioritise business actions.

## Automation Potential

High for consolidating analytical findings
and drafting management recommendations.

Medium for decision-making because operational
trade-offs and business priorities require
human judgement.

## Risks and Limitations

AI may generate unsupported recommendations,
misinterpret analytical findings, overlook
policy conflicts or assume decision authority
without management approval.
